PATT

A simple time tracker. Tap a task to time it, see what's left of today and this week, count your exercise and keep tabs on time-wasters. Free, with your own private account.

iPhone

Works like an app from your Home Screen.

  1. Open PATT in Safari (button below).
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open PATT from your Home Screen and tap Continue with Google.
Open PATT

Windows PC

Full screen panel, global hotkey, reports and timesheets.

  1. Download and run the installer (no admin needed).
  2. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. To use your phone too, click Sign in under the date and use the same Google account.
Download for Windows

Windows 10 or 11. Release notes

Mac or any computer

The same app in your browser: Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox.

  1. Open PATT (button below).
  2. Tap Continue with Google.
  3. Optional: bookmark it, or in Chrome use Install PATT from the address bar.
