Privacy

The short version: your data is yours, it's only used to run PATT for you, and nothing is sold or shown to advertisers.

What PATT stores

  • Your Google account's email address and a sign-in token. PATT never sees your Google password.
  • What you put in: tasks, targets, timer sessions, notes, moods, exercise, not-to-do slips and settings.

Where it's kept

  • On your PC: the Windows app keeps everything in a file on your computer (%LOCALAPPDATA%\PATT). If you never sign in, nothing leaves your PC.
  • In the cloud, once you sign in: a Supabase database in Sydney, Australia, so your phone, browser and PC stay in step. That's also why Google's sign-in screen mentions stggkftvuzwsserlervg.supabase.co, which is PATT's database address.

Who can see it

  • Only you, through the app. Each account can read and change only its own data.
  • Simon, who runs PATT, could technically open the database. He'll only do that to fix a problem you've told him about.

The not-to-do list on Windows

To spot time-wasters, PATT for Windows checks the title of the window you're using every few seconds and compares it with the words you chose (for example "youtube"). Titles are not stored or sent anywhere; only the slip itself (which item, when, how long) is saved.

No tracking

No ads and no analytics, and your data is never sold or shared.

Deleting your data

On the PC, uninstall PATT and delete the %LOCALAPPDATA%\PATT folder. To delete your cloud account and everything in it, ask Simon (the person who shared PATT with you) and he'll remove it.
